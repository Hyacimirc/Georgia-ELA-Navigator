Grade 9 · Practices · 9.P.EICC.1

Reader & Writer Identity

Official standard

Build an identity as a reader and writer, developing a repertoire of resources and tools to continuously expand participation as an active consumer and producer of texts.

Open official record ↗

KNOW

Audience, purpose, genre, strategies, writing process, feedback, and revision.

UNDERSTAND

Effective readers and writers make intentional choices and adapt their strategies.

DO

Set goals, choose strategies, reflect, use feedback, and demonstrate independence.

Suggested learning targets

  • I can set and monitor reading and writing goals.
  • I can choose strategies that support my purpose.
  • I can explain how feedback improves my work.

Suggested evidence of mastery

A portfolio showing goal setting, work samples, feedback, revision, and reflection.
